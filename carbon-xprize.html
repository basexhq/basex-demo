The value your projects create beyond carbon

A restored forest doesn't just draw down CO₂. It cleans the air. It casts shade that cools streets and crops. It shelters biodiversity and feeds pollinators. It holds water in the land — retention against drought, buffering against flood. It rebuilds living soil instead of depleting it. It cuts agricultural runoff, which means fewer dead zones downstream.

Every one of those is real value, delivered to real people. And today, almost all of it is priced at exactly zero.

What BaseX does

BaseX is price discovery for impact. Your project files a claim — carbon and co-benefits. Independent evaluators estimate its dollar value. A trimmed, confidence-weighted consensus emerges: one honest, challengeable number, with the uncertainty kept visible. Like a market price — except it counts what markets have been leaving out.
